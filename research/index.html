  • Majorana Physics in superconductors#

Topological superconductors, as an emergent class of superconducting states, provides an ideal platform for the investigation of topology as well as parity symmetry. They also support the existence of Majorana anyons, quasiparticles that are their own antiparticles, with potential applications in fault-tolerant quantum computation. Low temperature STM/STS are advanced experimental techniques widely used to study topological superconducting states due to their ultrahigh spatial and energy resolution. Our research is focusing on using advanced STM technique, such as Spin-polarized STM, to image the magnetic field response of such anyons in topological superconductor candidates. Further more, we seek the possibility to manipulates Majorana anyons and make fusions with them. This research is supported by the National Science Foundation of China.

  • Low-dimensional material fabrication#

In recent years, low-dimensional quantum materials have received extensive research attention due to their unique electronic properties and potential device applications. Compared with traditional electronic materials, low-dimensional quantum materials exhibit many novel electronic characteristics and advantages under the influence of dimensionality reduction and quantum confinement effect. For example, one-dimensional carbon nanotubes possess high carrier mobility and mechanical strength; two-dimensional graphene and TMD materials serve as ideal platforms for investigating novel electronic properties such as the quantum Hall effect and fractional quantum Hall effect; three-dimensional topological insulators feature topologically protected, spin-orbit locked surface states. The research on low-dimensional quantum materials can bring numerous potential technological advances to a wide range of fields spanning from computing to energy.

Currently, we are interested in three sub-directions:

  1. MBE growth of non-conventional magnetic thin film.
  2. Construction of hybrid system: By employing the proximitized effect, we wish to bring the advantage of each material together. We are especially interested in building hetero-structures based on a superconducting substrate.
  3. On-surface synthesis：Carbon magnetism offers a possibility of making spin logic devices based on organic material. Our research focuses on the synthesis and characterization of graphene nano structures, such as graphenen nanoribbon and nanograohenes.
