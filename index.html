Welcome to the Low-dimensional Quantum Material Group at the School of Physics, Beijing Institute of Technology.

Our research focuses on the fabrication of low-dimensional quantum material using Molecular Beam Epitaxial (MBE) and imaging the surface of those materials with Scanning Tunneling Microscope (STM) as well as Atom Force Microscope (AFM) technique. The materials platform we study includes topological materials (topological insulator, topological superconductor), magnetic materials (thin film magnet, non-colinear magnet, altermagnet), organic materials (graphene nanoribbon, nanographene) and hybrids materials (TI-SC, proximitized SC). We aim to explore the novel physical phenomenons in these materials and employ them into future advanced devices.
